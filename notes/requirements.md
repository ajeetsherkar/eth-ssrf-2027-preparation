# ETH SSRF 2027 — Application Requirements

## 1. Required Documents

- [ ] CV in English (Europass format).
- [ ] Official academic transcript from home university.
- [ ] Letter of motivation / personal statement in English.
- [ ] Upload all documents in PDF format.

## 2. Transcript Requirements

- Must be an official transcript issued by the university.
- Accepted languages: English, German, French or Italian.
- Self-made transcripts are not accepted.
- Screenshots of student portals, transcripts or certificates are not accepted.
- Translated transcripts must be supplied by the issuing university or an authorized professional translator.
- Use the grading system provided by your university.

## 3. Motivation Letter

- Maximum length: one A4 page.
- Must be written in English.
- Explain why you selected your proposed research area.
- Explain what you hope to accomplish at ETH.
- Explain how the fellowship supports your academic or professional goals and research career.

## 4. File and Personal Details

- PDF files only.
- Do not upload password-protected PDFs.
- Do not write your name in all capitals.
- Enter only your name in the name field.

## 5. Recommendation Letter

Recommendation letter required? Y / N / check

Note: The official ETH How to Apply page does not list a recommendation letter among its required documents. Verify again in the SiROP application form before submission because other sources may differ.

## 6. Application Details

- Application platform: SiROP.
- SiROP registration completed.
- University affiliation in SiROP is optional.
- Application deadline: 16 December 2026, 13:00 CET.
- Submit a complete application with all required documents.
